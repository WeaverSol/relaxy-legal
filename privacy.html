Privacy Policy

Effective date: 18 November 2026

Relaxy is a collection of relaxing mini-games and toys for Android, made by Weaversol ("we", "us"). This policy explains what information the app uses, why, and the choices you have. If you have any questions, email us at contact@weaversol.com.

The short version

  • You don't need an account. We never ask for your name, email address, photos, contacts or precise location.
  • Your scores, stars, unlocks, daily tasks and settings stay on your device. We don't run a server and we never receive them.
  • The app is free and shows ads from Google AdMob. Google may collect some device information to show and measure those ads, as explained below.
  • Players under 16 only get ads that are not personalized, and players under 13 only get ads suitable for children.

Information kept on your device

The app saves the following on your phone or tablet only:

  • Game progress: best scores, stars, levels reached, unlocked games and toys, daily tasks and your streak.
  • Settings: whether sound effects and vibration are on.
  • Year of birth: the year entered on the first screen (see "Children" below).

We cannot see or access this information. "Reset progress" in Settings clears your progress. Uninstalling the app, or clearing its storage in Android settings, removes everything.

Ball Maze and Dice Roll can use your device's motion sensor so you can tilt the maze or shake the dice. The readings are used instantly on the device and are never stored or sent anywhere.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The app is paid for by ads provided by Google AdMob, a service of Google. To show ads, measure how they perform and prevent fraud, the Google Mobile Ads software in the app may collect the following and send it to Google:

  • device identifiers, such as the Android advertising ID, where allowed (see "Children" below);
  • your IP address, from which an approximate location (such as country or city) can be estimated;
  • device and app information, such as device model, operating system version, language and app version;
  • ad activity, such as which ads were shown or tapped, and diagnostic information such as crash reports from the ad software.

Google uses this information as described in its privacy policy (https://policies.google.com/privacy) and in "How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services" (https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites). The information is sent over an encrypted connection.

We do not sell personal information. We do not receive the information Google collects in a form that identifies you; we only see summary reports, such as how many ads were shown.

Your ad choices

  • If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, Switzerland or another place where the law requires it, the app asks for your consent with Google's consent form before personalized ads can be shown. You can change your choice at any time with "Ad privacy options" in Settings.
  • On Android you can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of personalized ads, in your device's settings (usually Settings > Privacy > Ads, or Google > Ads).
  • Watching a rewarded ad (for example, to unlock a game early) is always optional.

Children

Relaxy is made for everyone, including children. The first time the app opens it asks for the player's year of birth. The answer stays on the device and is never sent to us or to Google. It is used only to choose how ads are requested:

  • Under 13: every ad request is marked as directed to a child (as required by the US Children's Online Privacy Protection Act, COPPA, and similar laws). Google then turns off personalized ads and remarketing for those requests, and ads are limited to content suitable for general audiences.
  • 13 to 15: ad requests are marked as coming from someone under the age of consent, so personalized ads are not shown.

We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. Because we don't hold any information about any player, there is nothing on our side to review or delete. A parent or guardian can remove all data by uninstalling the app or clearing its storage, and is welcome to contact us at contact@weaversol.com.

Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR in Europe, the UK GDPR, or the CCPA in California), you may have the right to access, correct or delete your personal information, to restrict or object to its use, and to withdraw consent. Where the GDPR applies, personalized ads rely on your consent; Google is responsible for its own processing of ad data and explains its legal bases in its privacy policy.

The information the app keeps is on your device and fully under your control. For information Google collects for ads, use the choices above or Google's tools at https://myadcenter.google.com. You can also contact us, or complain to your local data protection authority.

How long information is kept

Information on your device is kept until you reset your progress, clear the app's storage or uninstall the app. Google keeps ad information for the periods described in its own policies.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update it in the app and change the effective date above. If a change is significant, we will tell you in the app.
